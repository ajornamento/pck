Pax Christi International (PCI) is an international Catholic peace movement dedicated to nonviolence, peace, and reconciliation. Founded in 1945, PCI unites all members of the Catholic Church as equals and is active in more than 50 countries with over 120 member organizations worldwide.

Grounded in the Gospel and the Catholic faith, PCI pursues a vision of "a peaceful world, free from all violence" through the method of prayer, study, and action.

At the global, regional, and national levels, PCI's member organizations respond — independently or in solidarity with one another — to local and global challenges, leading efforts in nonviolent action, human rights, human security, disarmament and demilitarization, a just international order, and interreligious peace. PCI also holds representative status with international bodies such as the United Nations, UNESCO, the African Union, and the Council of Europe.

Pax Christi Korea

Pax Christi Korea (PCK) is the Korean chapter of Pax Christi International, established in 2019 to carry PCI's mission into the realities of the Korean Peninsula.

PCK works for peace grounded in justice — supporting nonviolence, reconciliation, and solidarity, with a particular focus on overcoming the division of the Korean Peninsula and building peace across East Asia.
